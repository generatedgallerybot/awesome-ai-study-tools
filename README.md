## Flashcard Generators

- [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/) - Open-source spaced repetition flashcard app with a massive shared deck library. The gold standard for memorization.
- [Quizlet](https://quizlet.com/) - Popular flashcard platform with AI-powered study modes, learn mode, and millions of user-created sets.
- [Knowt](https://knowt.com/) - AI-powered flashcard generator that creates cards from your notes and videos. Free for students.
- [Wisdolia](https://wisdolia.com/) - Chrome extension that generates flashcards from any webpage, article, or PDF using AI.
- [Mochi](https://mochi.cards/) - Markdown-based spaced repetition app with clean UI and Anki import support.

## Textbook Summarizers

- [Scholarcy](https://www.scholarcy.com/) - AI article summarizer that breaks down research papers into structured summaries with key findings.
- [SciSummary](https://scisummary.com/) - Summarizes scientific articles via email — forward a paper and get a plain-English summary back.
- [Textbooks.ai](https://textbooks.ai/) - AI-powered textbook companion that generates chapter summaries, practice questions, and study guides from any textbook.
- [Explainpaper](https://www.explainpaper.com/) - Upload a paper, highlight confusing text, and get AI explanations in simple language.
- [TLDR This](https://tldrthis.com/) - Web-based summarizer for articles, papers, and documents with adjustable summary length.

## Exam Prep

- [Studocu](https://www.studocu.com/) - Student document sharing platform with AI-generated practice questions and summaries from uploaded notes.
- [PrepAI](https://www.prepai.io/) - Generates practice questions (MCQ, short answer, true/false) from any text or document.
- [Questgen](https://www.questgen.ai/) - AI question generator for educators and students — creates quizzes from any content.
- [Revisely](https://revisely.com/) - AI-powered revision tool that generates flashcards and quizzes from your study materials.

## AI Tutors

- [Khanmigo](https://www.khanacademy.org/khan-labs) - Khan Academy's AI tutor powered by GPT-4. Guides students through problems step-by-step without giving away answers.
- [Socratic by Google](https://socratic.org/) - Point your camera at a homework question and get step-by-step explanations using Google AI.
- [Synthesis Tutor](https://www.synthesis.is/) - AI math tutor with adaptive problem sets and real-time feedback for K-12 students.
- [Elicit](https://elicit.com/) - AI research assistant that helps find, summarize, and synthesize academic papers.

## Note Taking

- [Notion AI](https://www.notion.so/product/ai) - AI writing assistant built into Notion. Summarize notes, generate outlines, brainstorm, and extract action items.
- [Otter.ai](https://otter.ai/) - AI meeting and lecture transcription with automated summaries, action items, and searchable notes.
- [AudioPen](https://audiopen.ai/) - Turn rambling voice notes into clean, structured text using AI. Great for capturing lecture thoughts.
- [Mem](https://get.mem.ai/) - AI-powered note-taking app that self-organizes and surfaces relevant notes when you need them.

## All-in-One Platforms

- [Quizlet Plus](https://quizlet.com/plus) - Premium tier with AI-powered custom practice tests, expert solutions, and enhanced study modes.
- [Brainscape](https://www.brainscape.com/) - Adaptive flashcard platform using Confidence-Based Repetition for optimized learning.
- [RemNote](https://www.remnote.com/) - Knowledge management tool combining note-taking, flashcards, and spaced repetition in one app.
